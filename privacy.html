CCC Financial Freedom — Privacy Policy

Operator: William Hix | willhix98@msn.com | Version 1.0 | Effective 2026-11-23

1. Who we are

CCC Financial Freedom ("the application") is a private personal finance ledger operated by William Hix ("the Operator") for his own household. Contact: willhix98@msn.com.

2. What data we collect through Plaid

When you link a financial account, Plaid Inc. provides the application with read-only data about that account: the institution and account names and types, balances, transactions, investment holdings, loan and credit-card liability details, and the last four digits of account numbers. The application does not receive or store your bank login credentials, full account or routing numbers, or identity documents. Plaid's own handling of your data is governed by Plaid's End User Privacy Policy (https://plaid.com/legal/#end-user-privacy-policy).

3. How we use it

To maintain the household's financial ledger: net worth, cash flow, bills and due dates, savings and allocation targets, and budgeting guidance from an in-app assistant. Data is used for no other purpose.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell, rent, license, share or otherwise disclose your data to any third party. We do not use it for advertising or marketing. We do not initiate payments, transfers or any transaction through Plaid.

5. Where it is stored and how it is protected

Data is stored in a hosted PostgreSQL database encrypted at rest and reachable only from the Operator's server, which itself accepts connections only from the local machine. Plaid access tokens are additionally encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key held outside the database. All connections use TLS. Administrative accounts are protected by multi-factor authentication. Encrypted backups are kept locally and in an off-site object store.

6. Retention and deletion

Data for a linked account is retained while the account remains linked. Unlinking an account removes it at Plaid and deletes or closes its rows in the ledger. The Operator can delete all data at any time. Backups age out under a fixed retention schedule. You may request deletion or a copy of your data by emailing the contact above; requests are honored within 30 days.

7. Consent

Linking an account through Plaid Link is your consent to the collection, processing and storage described here. You may withdraw consent at any time by unlinking the account or by emailing the contact above.

8. Changes

This policy is reviewed at least annually and updated when the application's handling of financial data changes. Version 1.0, effective 2026-11-23.
